About me

Careful with details, curious about patterns.

I study Artificial Intelligence in my third year at the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence, Beni-Suef University.

I enjoy the part of data work where a messy spreadsheet becomes a story: cleaning columns, finding the pattern, then choosing the one chart that shows it. I also design relational databases, from the ERD to the mapping schema.

When I hit a problem, I break it into small checks, test each one, and write down what I found. I have learned SQL for querying data, and I am now learning Power BI and Machine Learning to take on bigger datasets.
